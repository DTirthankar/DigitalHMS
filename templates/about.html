About MediLinkAI

An overview of MediLinkAI — an AI-powered digital healthcare platform connecting patients with healthcare services, emergency support, and medical assistance through one unified system.

Overview

Smart Healthcare, Connected for Everyone

MediLinkAI is an AI-powered digital healthcare platform designed to make healthcare services more accessible, connected, and easier to reach. It brings multiple healthcare facilities and support services together through one unified platform.

The platform combines AI-powered disease triage and urgency assessment with nearby hospital and doctor discovery, specialist referral, emergency SOS support, diagnostic and laboratory services, medicine availability, blood bank information, and other healthcare support services.

MediLinkAI is designed to help patients find the right healthcare support based on their needs. When a patient is unable to travel during an emergency, the platform can support emergency doctor visit coordination and ambulance assistance where available. Patients can order medicines through MediLinkAI from nearby participating medical shops where the required medicine is available. Similar to a food-delivery marketplace, the platform connects the patient with the available medical shop and arranges delivery to the patient's location, with an intended delivery time of up to 1 hour depending on medicine availability, shop location, and delivery availability.

The AI triage component provides preliminary disease predictions and urgency assessment from patient-provided symptoms. The machine learning component uses a Random Forest model trained on a structured dataset of 4,920 patient records covering 41 disease categories and 132 symptom features.

MediLinkAI is intended as a healthcare support and connectivity platform and does not replace professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical services.

Scope

Limitations & Future Development

This study is subject to several limitations that should be considered when interpreting results or extending this work to applied settings.

Synthetic balance: the dataset contains exactly 120 records per class, which does not reflect real-world disease prevalence distributions. Classifier performance on imbalanced clinical data may differ significantly.
Binary symptom encoding: symptoms are represented as present or absent, discarding symptom severity, duration, and co-occurrence dynamics that may carry clinical significance.
No clinical validation: the system has not been validated against real patient outcomes or reviewed by clinical professionals. Results should not be applied in any diagnostic context.

Future directions include integration of continuous biomarker features, deep learning approaches for higher-dimensional input spaces, and prospective validation against de-identified clinical records under appropriate ethical review.
