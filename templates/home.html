Overview

What is ChronicML?

Chronic diseases such as heart disease, cancer, and diabetes are the leading causes of death and disability worldwide. They are also leading drivers of rising healthcare costs globally.

ChronicML is an academic research project that applies supervised machine learning to automatically classify diseases based on patient-reported symptoms — helping demonstrate the feasibility of AI-assisted preliminary screening.

Three in four adults have at least one chronic condition. Early and accurate detection remains one of the most impactful ways to reduce long-term health burden.

Key contributions

What this research addresses

Combining clinical symptomatology with supervised learning to support early-stage disease identification.

⚛

Multi-class classification

Classifies symptom vectors across 41 distinct disease categories using five machine learning algorithms including Random Forest, SVM, and Naïve Bayes.

▤

Structured symptom encoding

132 clinical symptoms encoded as binary features, enabling standardised input representation and reproducible experimental conditions across all models.

▲

Comparative evaluation

Five classifiers benchmarked under identical train-test splits and 5-fold cross-validation, providing rigorous and reproducible performance comparison.
